# Content Rights Checklist

Before publishing an asset, record its creator, source URL or contract reference, license, commercial-use permission, modification permission, attribution requirements, review date, and redistribution rights.

For quotes, record exact wording, source used to verify attribution, language, and review status.

Safer options include commissioned original anime-inspired artwork and assets licensed for commercial distribution inside an app. Do not assume fan art, screenshots, posters, or images found online are reusable. Franchise names, recognizable characters, logos, and quote collections may raise separate rights questions. This checklist is not legal advice.
